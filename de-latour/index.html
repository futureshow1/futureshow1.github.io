Work with Agata

Three ways we can work together.

Training

Your team already has the material. It is the three or four incidents nobody brings up: the client who would not deal with a woman, the meeting where the interpreter softened something, the colleague who stopped speaking up in March. I collect them anonymously two weeks beforehand, and we spend the day on those.

Fororganisations working with newcomers, local government, schools, multinational teams

Facilitation

Some meetings cannot be chaired, only held. Consultations where the decision is already resented, a team meeting after a conflict, two sides at one table who each believe the other has already won. I prepare with each side separately, run the day, and write the note afterwards in language both sides can sign.

Forpublic institutions, organisations in crisis, teams after a change

Advisory

Your form is the first conversation your institution has with a person. Very often it is also the last. I go through the materials, the language, the procedures and the whole path someone takes to reach you, and come back with a list of what turns people away and what can be changed inside a month.

Forpublic administration, universities, integration programmes, European projects

How I got here

I left Poland in my twenties, and learned the world by comparing it.

Living between Polish and French, you spend years noticing what travels between two cultures and what quietly does not. I made a profession of it: first as a journalist and translator in Brussels, then as a sworn interpreter in courtrooms — where a sentence is not finished until someone has said it again in the other language and both sides accept that this is what was said.

That is where the real work turned out to be. Not vocabulary: knowing what a person meant, what they said instead, and which of the two to carry across.

In 2016 the Council of Europe asked me to research and edit its handbook on answering hate speech. I spent a year reading the worst things people say to each other, and the best replies anyone had found. The most useful thing I learned was not what to say back. It was when not to.

Since then I have worked with teams receiving refugees, city departments whose forms nobody returns, and organisations where something was said in a meeting two years ago and has not been mentioned since. Working with people who travelled much further than I did — from the Middle East, from Central and South Asia — has widened all of it again.

I do not make the difference disappear. I make it workable.

Zmiana względem poprzedniej wersji: oś to teraz emigracja i adaptacyjność, tak jak prosiłaś, a nie tłumaczenie przysięgłe — ono zostało, ale jako część drogi. Brakuje jednego faktu: kiedy wyjechałaś i w jakim wieku. Pierwsze zdanie jest na razie przybliżone.

You do not build peace by avoiding conflict. You build it by making a room safe enough to go through one.

The classic approach sorts people by where they come from and predicts how they will behave. It is a useful first glance and a poor second one. It fixes people in place, and it leaves no room for the only thing that actually interests me: how a particular person behaves in a particular room, on a particular day, with these particular people.

So I work from curiosity rather than categories. The frameworks are there — I know them well enough to teach them — but they stay in service of the situation in front of us, never the other way round.

The method

What can be said here

Three movements, whether it is a two-day course or one difficult meeting. Deliberately broad: the shape stays the same, what goes inside it is yours.

01

Find out what cannot be said yet

Before anything is practised, I find out which sentences are currently impossible in this room, and why. It is rarely the topic in the brief.

02

Say one true thing on purpose

Not everything. One thing, said deliberately, in front of everyone, at a moment the group can survive it. That is the whole technique, and it takes preparation.

03

Leave a rule behind, not a feeling

A workshop that ends in goodwill has changed nothing by Thursday. We leave with one sentence, one procedure or one change to a form — something that works when I am not there.

The method is a frame, not a doctrine. It holds a two-hour conversation and a four-month programme equally well, and it does not tell anyone in advance who they are.
